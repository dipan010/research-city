Projects

Do complaints follow the money?

Joins 618,202 Bengaluru citizen grievances to ₹23,241 crore of BBMP work orders across 198 wards. The two only loosely correspond, and the geography of both turns out to be a property of the denominator rather than the city. Raw counts favour the periphery, per square kilometre favours the core, per resident favours the periphery again: the same two columns, three opposite answers. A third of the spending belongs to no ward at all.

Mumbai's toilets are where the slums are

Overlays 2,541 slum cluster polygons on ten of BMC's amenity layers. Slum land turns out to be closer to every service than the rest of the city, in 21 of 21 wards for public toilets, because half of Mumbai's toilet seats stand on 7% of its land. The sanitation deficit is real but invisible to a distance measure: it is 14 households sharing every seat.
